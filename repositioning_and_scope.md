# Reborn Tech — ITAD Repositioning and Website Scope

## Decision

**Reborn Tech should be positioned as an ITAD and circular-technology partner, not as a handset-repair business.** Repair remains important, but only as one outcome after assets have been securely collected, recorded, erased, tested and triaged. The central promise is that retired IT can be handled securely, responsibly and visibly—from the first collection scan through to reuse, recycling or charitable impact.

> **Proposed public proposition:** “Secure ITAD. Proven second life.”

This differentiates Reborn from a generic recycler and avoids reducing the business to repair. It also leaves space for Bulk GSM’s established capabilities: collection, asset tracking, data erasure, grading, valuation, buyback, refurbishment, WEEE recycling, reporting and downstream impact.

## Service architecture

The public offer should be structured around what a client needs to achieve, not around internal departments.

| Service pillar | The customer problem | Reborn Tech response | Evidence to show |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan & Collect** | Retired IT is dispersed, disruptive to remove and carries risk before collection. | Asset scoping, site planning, secure transport and signed transfer of custody. | Collection brief, chain of custody and waste-transfer documentation where applicable. |
| **Secure & Record** | Organisations need accountability for every device and its data. | Serial / IMEI / asset-tag capture, secure facility intake and configured data-erasure route. | Asset register, data-erasure certificate and audit trail. |
| **Assess & Recover Value** | Useful hardware should not be treated as scrap. | Testing, grading, valuation, buyback and refurbishability assessment. | Valuation outcome and disposition decision. |
| **Redeploy & Create Impact** | Organisations want reused IT to create a useful outcome, not just leave their estate. | Remarketing, approved redistribution routes and, where agreed, charitable / community impact paths. | Outcome reporting, approved partner evidence and impact summary. |
| **Recycle Responsibly** | Non-reusable assets need compliant final treatment. | WEEE-compliant downstream recycling and material recovery. | Disposal certificates and recycling report. |

The homepage should function as a concise proof journey rather than a generic list of services.

| Chapter | Visitor takeaway | Suggested content and interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **2. The trace** | Reborn knows exactly what happens next. | A horizontally offset five-stage route: collect, secure, assess, redistribute, recover. |
| **3. The proof** | ITAD can be auditable without creating friction. | Asset-level tracking, certificate and reporting tiles; factual claims held behind approval gates. |

| **6. The next action** | The client knows how to start. | ITAD assessment form pathway, site-visit preparation, two operational hubs and future portal entry. |

## Cinematic impact story

The core video should be an **asset-to-impact narrative**, designed for the hero and a stand-alone campaign cut. It should avoid sentimental shorthand and make each transition credible.

| Story beat | Visual action | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Collection** | A labelled crate is collected from an office or loading bay; transfer documentation is signed. | Accountability begins at handover. |
| **Sorting** | Devices move through a real warehouse environment; a laptop is separated from material-recovery items. | Each asset receives an outcome, not a blanket disposal route. |
| **Secure processing** | A technician records a device, runs the approved data-erasure process and checks the record. | Data security is active and evidenced. |
| **Repair / renewal** | A technician restores a viable device; close detail on careful, physical craft. | Reuse is a technical process, not a vague promise. |
| **Materials recovery** | Non-reusable components are correctly separated and prepared for downstream recycling. | Recycling remains essential for the right assets. |
| **Redistribution** | A clean box containing a renewed laptop reaches a partner programme. | The asset’s second life has a destination. |
| **Human outcome** | A child, student or community member receives and starts using the device in an authentic setting. | Retired IT can become access, confidence and opportunity. |

The final human scene is the emotional peak, but it must be handled responsibly. It should depict a verified partner route and consented subject where it uses real footage. Until then, use an illustrative or non-identifying representation rather than suggesting a donation that has already occurred.

## Future portal model — provision only

The public website should reserve a clear **Customer Portal** entry in the header and footer. In the first release it should open a transparent “coming soon / request access” message rather than a fake dashboard or simulated login.

| Role | Intended scope |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer Administrator** | Manage organisation users, sites, requests and document visibility. |
| **Customer Site Contact** | Complete site-visit details, prepare collections and view assigned jobs. |
| **Customer Compliance Viewer** | Access certificates, asset reports and exports without operational permissions. |
| **Reborn Operations** | Scope, schedule, collect and update job status. |
| **Reborn Compliance** | Configure data requirements, issue documents and manage evidence quality. |
| **Reborn Administrator** | Maintain customer accounts, role permissions, reporting settings and partner workflows. |

The separate portal project should begin from the same entity hierarchy: **organisation → site → collection / job → asset record → evidence / certificate → outcome / impact report**.

## Non-negotiable proof gates

The copy must avoid risky claims until each is confirmed with supporting evidence and approval.

| Topic | Current source indication | Publication rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Securaze / erasure claims** | Brochure references Securaze and tamper-proof erasure certificates. | Confirm current partnership, approved name, scope and logo rights. |
| **ADISA / Common Criteria / NIST / GDPR language** | Referenced across supplied material. | Confirm certifications, specific standards, validity and legal review. Do not imply certification where processes are only aligned. |
| **Environment Agency waste-carrier status** | Depicted in the process document. | Publish current licence number and accurate legal entity only once verified. |
| **Impact numbers** | Second Life material includes high-level volume claims. | Do not use totals without dated source, methodology and approval. |
| **StayWell partnership** | Supplied material names StayWell; the public site confirms wellbeing activity. | Confirm commercial mechanism, exact copy, charity / brand assets and reporting approach. |

## Recommended build boundary for release one

Release one should deliver the **public ITAD story, enquiry conversion, operational proof structure and a future-ready portal entry point**. It should not include authentication, user accounts, live reporting dashboards, portal data, live donation counters, unverified certificates or invented testimonials.
