# Reborn Tech — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches considered

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Material Trace** | A cinematic industrial-editorial direction that follows an asset’s journey from collection to renewed purpose. It balances operational credibility with human consequence. | 0.07 |
| **Proof in Motion** | A clean, high-trust systems design where evidence, certification and reporting take visual precedence. It feels precise, regulated and enterprise-oriented. | 0.03 |
| **Second Life Journal** | A warmer, documentary-led visual language built around people, community partners and the emotional outcomes of reuse. It would feel open, generous and humane. | 0.09 |

## Chosen approach: Material Trace

### Design Movement

**Industrial editorialism with cinematic documentary framing.** Reborn Tech should feel like an organisation that understands the physical reality of IT assets: crates, serial numbers, wipe stations, parts, materials, warehouse routes and carefully handed-over equipment. The visual storytelling will counterbalance this operational rigour with the visible downstream human value of reuse and charitable impact.

### Core Principles

1. **Show the journey, not just the claim.** The interface should make each stage in the ITAD chain legible: collection, inventory, data security, triage, reuse, materials recovery, redistribution and impact reporting.
2. **Make operational proof feel tangible.** Precise labels, restrained data cues, serial-like typographic details and clear process sequencing should convey control and accountability without overloading the visitor.
3. **Use human outcome as the emotional payoff.** People should not be decorative stock imagery. The journey culminates in a meaningful outcome: a working device in the hands of a person or community who can benefit from it.
4. **Be serious, never sterile.** The site will be premium and direct, avoiding eco clichés, vague promises and superficial green imagery.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is **charcoal graphite** and **warm paper**, echoing device housings, packing materials and audit documentation. A restrained, unmistakable **Signal Lime** is the active brand colour: it represents the moment an old asset re-enters useful circulation. It will be used sparingly for trace lines, operational status markers and primary calls to action, rather than large decorative fields. Deep mineral green acts as the secondary sustainability tone; it should read as composed and credible, not generic “eco.”

### Layout Paradigm

The homepage is a **vertical asset journey**, not a conventional stacked marketing grid. Each chapter behaves as a different physical moment: an opening manifesto over a cinematic visual, an offset tracking rail for process stages, a split field for service evidence, a data-strip for assurance, and a final full-bleed impact chapter. Content will pull left and right along a subtle route line, suggesting an asset progressing through Reborn rather than simply scrolling through cards.

### Signature Elements

1. **The Trace Line:** A fine Signal Lime route line, occasionally annotated with process marks and locations, guides the journey through each major chapter.
2. **Asset Labels:** Compact monospaced operational labels—such as `COLLECTED`, `SECURED`, `REDEPLOYED`—introduce content sections and establish a traceability motif.
3. **Impact Frames:** Large documentary image frames that crop close to physical details at the start of the journey, then widen to reveal people and outcomes at the end.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like examining a well-run operation. Hovering a service stage reveals a concise proof point; buttons provide firm feedback without theatrical effects; the process line visibly progresses as a user scrolls. Future portal entry points will be clear and functional, not hidden behind marketing language. Any unbuilt portal access will be identified as a placeholder rather than implied as a live service.

### Animation

Motion will be restrained and purposeful. On entry, image frames lift by a few pixels while the Trace Line draws in; sequential process labels appear in a short stagger. The hero will use a cinematically paced visual treatment, with a transparent blurred content panel that preserves the underlying story while maintaining reliable legibility. Scroll-linked effects must be optional and disabled for people who prefer reduced motion. Buttons compress very slightly on press and change state in under 160ms.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** will carry headlines, navigation and service names: structured, technical and distinctive without feeling cold. **Source Serif 4** will add editorial weight to longer impact statements and selected pull quotes. **IBM Plex Mono** will be reserved for operational labels, small metadata and locations. Headlines should be decisive and short; body text should be compact, readable and evidence-led. The design will not use Inter.

### Brand Essence

**Reborn Tech gives organisations a secure, measurable way to turn retired IT into verified second life and real-world value.**

Personality: **assured, traceable, human**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines should name the transformation plainly. CTAs should invite a practical next step and never depend on empty sustainability language. Microcopy should convey control, transparency and partnership.

> “Your old IT still has somewhere to go.”

> “Start an asset journey.”

### Approved headline direction

The final working tagline is **“Secure ITAD. Second life, verified.”** It retains the two concepts Kavi selected while avoiding the overly abbreviated “Secure ITAD + 2nd Life.” The short sentence gives the second-life claim an operational standard: it has to be traceable and evidenced.

### Wordmark & Logo

The logo mark is an **open-loop circuit arrow**: a bold, simplified loop that suggests both a route through a processing chain and a second-life cycle, with one purposeful break to imply human intervention. The wordmark should use a custom-spaced, compact grotesk treatment rather than a default font lock-up. The icon must work alone in the header, favicon and portal entry.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Lime — `#C9F14A`**. It is Reborn’s unmistakable signal that an asset has returned to useful circulation.

## Build guardrails

- The public website is **ITAD-first**, with device repair presented as one operational route inside a broader reuse and recovery system rather than the brand’s primary identity.
- Reborn is a joint venture of Bulk GSM and industry veterans Andi and Jason. It operates across London (NW10 2XA) and Chorley (Unit 21, Coppull Mill, PR7 5BW); content will state this accurately once operational material is confirmed.
- The customer portal and role-based admin area are intentionally **provisioned in the information architecture** but will be built separately. Live authentication, role rules and customer data handling are not to be implied in the first public-site release.
- Supporting StayWell should be framed as a genuine partnership only after the donation mechanism, approved language and reporting evidence are confirmed.
- Every significant design decision should reinforce the **Material Trace** philosophy rather than add generic technology or sustainability decoration.

## Style Decisions

- Signal Lime `#C9F14A` is reserved for trace lines, operational markers, primary actions, key numerals and compact proof accents. It must not appear as a full-bleed section field.
- Every major homepage chapter carries at least one traceability cue: an asset label, route mark, process number, evidence strip or chain-of-custody annotation.
- The Reborn Tech mark is a bold open-loop circuit arrow paired with a compact grotesk wordmark. The loop and route geometry recur subtly beyond the header as part of the page system.
