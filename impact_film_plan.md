# Reborn Tech — Asset-to-Impact Film Treatment

## Global definition

The film is a **35-second, 16:9 cinematic documentary concept film** for Reborn Tech’s website. It will autoplay silently in the hero; sound is not required for the website experience. The visual treatment is naturalistic industrial editorial rather than glossy technology advertising: graphite equipment, real warehouse texture, mineral-green shadows, warm daylight and one controlled Signal Lime detail. No captions, logos, labels or on-screen text will be baked into the footage.

The narrative is for procurement, IT, facilities and ESG decision-makers. It demonstrates that the ordinary decision to retire a device can become a secure, visible and genuinely useful outcome. The emotional arc is controlled rather than sentimental: operational confidence gives way to human consequence.

| Shared element | Visual definition | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The asset crate** | A clean, hard-sided black ITAD transport crate containing orderly retired business laptops; a subtle non-branded lime tamper seal. | Establishes collection and traces the asset through the first two clips. |
| **The technician** | UK-based adult technician, late 30s to 40s, charcoal practical work jacket, no logos, calm professional handling. | Gives the secure-processing and repair sequences continuity. |
| **Signal Lime** | One small physical status/tamper detail only, never a field colour. | Operational status cue. |
| **Human outcome** | A local programme facilitator and a young student in a bright community learning space. | Final payoff; dignified, specific and non-sentimental. |

## Website implementation

The hero will load the five clips as one logical **film sequence**: each segment advances on video end and the full route loops from collection. A poster frame preserves a strong experience on slow connections or where motion is reduced. The page will never claim the depicted redistribution is a documented Reborn distribution; the film is explicitly an illustrative visual treatment until real partner footage and consented impact evidence are available.
